Vitamin Deficiency

Overview

Vitamin deficiency occurs when your body doesn't get enough of a certain vitamin, leading to health problems. Deficiencies can be caused by poor diet, absorption issues, or medical conditions. Common deficiencies include Vitamin D, B12, and Iron. Symptoms vary depending on the vitamin lacking, but often include fatigue, weakness, and skin or vision issues. Identifying the type of deficiency early and correcting it through diet or supplements can prevent long-term complications and improve overall well-being.

Symptoms

Common symptoms of vitamin deficiency include:

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Pale or yellowish skin
  • Muscle weakness
  • Mood changes or irritability
  • Hair loss
  • Vision problems
  • Numbness or tingling in hands and feet
  • Slow wound healing
  • Cracked lips or mouth ulcers
